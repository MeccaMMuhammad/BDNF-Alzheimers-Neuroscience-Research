# 2026 Research Update — What Published Studies Show

**Author:** Mecca Muhammad

**Update:** November 14, 2026  
**Status:** Retrospective literature review; no new laboratory experiments or experimental outcomes.

## Key takeaways

- **AAV-BDNF remyelination in cuprizone-treated mice (2025):** Vector delivery into the corpus callosum was associated with more oligodendrocyte maturation-related markers and improved myelin ultrastructure. **Not an Alzheimer's/APP-PS1 model.** [1]
- **AAV-BDNF in APP/PS1 and other AD models (2025):** A study observed transcriptomic responses after gene delivery. **It did not establish remyelination.** [2]
- **Oligodendrocyte-targeted SGK1.1 in 5×FAD AD mice (2026):** Reported improved myelin-related outcomes. **Different molecular intervention and different AD model.** [3]

## What remains unresolved

Whether AAV-mediated BDNF delivery causes demonstrable oligodendrocyte maturation and newly formed myelin in **APP/PS1 Alzheimer’s mice** is *not answered by these three selected studies*. Novelty cannot be established without a comprehensive search. Animal experiments and clinical benefit are not claimed.
